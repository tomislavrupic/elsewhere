# Rooms beyond the map

A fictional worked illustration. EXPLORE the impossibility faithfully.

## SEED → OPEN

```text
SEED("A civilization builds structures larger on the inside than the geography containing them.")
OPEN(seed, distance=elsewhere)
```

Keep the premise literal inside this world. Do not substitute an illusion or an
ordinary engineering explanation. Open the relations between exterior address,
interior extent, access, and civic responsibility.

## BRANCH

- **Address republic.** A doorway occupies one parcel but admits a whole province.
  Property is counted by entrances; services are allocated by interior population.
  A single street can require more water than the exterior continent can supply.
- **Custodial city.** Each interior belongs to whoever maintains its route back
  outside. Ownership is a responsibility for return, not possession of volume.
  Losing a route can strand a population without changing the exterior map.
- **Interior frontier.** Interiors contain their own towns and building traditions.
  New interior buildings exceed their containing geography in turn. Maps must
  state which entrance they describe; apparent proximity outside says little
  about a journey inside.

These are different governing structures, not three styles of gigantic palace.
Within each branch, keep access rules stable and expose tensions they create.

## DISTURB

Remove the assumption that each interior has a permanent entrance. Doorways
can migrate or disappear while inhabited interiors persist. This is an added
fictional rule, not a repair of the original impossibility.

The address republic loses its property identifiers. The custodial city's
maintenance obligation becomes urgent, but sometimes impossible to meet.
The frontier develops traveling registries: witnesses record the last usable
route, with an explicit possibility that the record is obsolete.

## FOLD — COLLAPSE

Exterior extent fails as a measure of civic responsibility. **Access routes
organize obligations more strongly than apparent size.** Preserve the difference
between recording a route, controlling a route, and guaranteeing return: a
registry cannot substitute for a usable door.

## RECOVER → SEED′

```text
SEED("Who is responsible for a place whose entrance has disappeared?")
```

The next seed carries an access failure and a responsibility question. It can
remain worldbuilding or transfer into design. Such a transfer would be an analogy,
not evidence that these interiors exist.

A possible next OPEN: obligations that remain after access is lost; archives
whose custodians cannot retrieve them; treaties with unreachable signatories.
The fictional world's impossibility remains intact.
