# Love

A worked illustration, not an observed model test. Mode: EXPLORE.

## SEED

```text
SEED("love")
OPEN(seed, distance=far)
```

Do not define the word once and close it. Open relations: recognition without
fusion; support without repayment; growth that changes the supporter; attention
whose timing matters more than its quantity.

## BRANCH and TRANSFER

1. **Architecture — a shared threshold.** Two homes retain separately controlled
   entrances but share shelter between them. Recognition becomes provision for
   another occupant; distinction becomes separate control. The threshold can
   hold an encounter without requiring either person to enter the other's home.
2. **Ecology — a succession habitat.** One organism's shelter enables another
   species to establish itself; the successor changes the habitat and may
   displace its supporter. Care becomes enabling conditions, not guaranteed
   mutual benefit. This is a design analogy, not a claim about biological motives.
3. **Music — an unfinished reply.** One phrase leaves a gap another can answer.
   The reply can change the first phrase's later return. Relation appears in
   timing and transformation, without requiring unison.
4. **Visual worldbuilding — a city of retained paths.** Residents build paths
   toward one another while keeping private exits. A path records an invitation;
   an unused path can weather without becoming an accusation.

```text
TRANSFER(seed, domain=architecture,
         preserve="recognition with distinct agency")
```

The branches differ in mechanism: access control, succession, temporal response,
and durable invitation. Heart-shaped buildings or sentimental color palettes
alone would be cosmetic transfer.

## DISTURB

```text
DISTURB(branches, remove=reciprocity,
        preserve="the recipient may decline")
```

The second home never opens its door. The successor never supports its predecessor.
The answering phrase never arrives. The path remains unused.

Architecture must still shelter without trapping. The habitat reveals a limit:
enabling another can destroy the conditions of future support. Music shifts from
conversation to a phrase capable of holding an unanswered interval. The city's
path needs expiration or maintenance rules so invitation does not become debt.

Do not rescue reciprocity by calling a future return "gratitude."

## FOLD — COLLAPSE

Across the branches, support changes another's available actions. The useful
distinction is **enabling versus demanding participation**. Retain a second
difference: ongoing material support can be exhausted; a musical gap does not
incur the same maintenance cost. "Love is generosity" would erase this difference.

## Optional AUDIT

Attack the candidate relation: a shared threshold can still pressure an occupant;
a path's visibility can enforce obligation. An exit must remain usable, not merely
pictured. The ecology branch supplies a counterexample to indefinitely sustainable
support. None of these representations establishes a universal definition of love.

## RECOVER → SEED′

```text
RECOVER(folded) → "support that leaves an exit"
SEED("support that leaves an exit")
```

This seed retains the agency distinction and invites a practical disturbance:
what happens when providing an exit consumes the resources needed for support?
The next traversal can branch into care systems, reversible contracts, or public
space. The original word did not specify that tradeoff.
