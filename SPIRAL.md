# The spiral

```text
S₀ → OPEN → BRANCH → DISTURB → FOLD → RECOVER → S₁
S₁ → OPEN → BRANCH → DISTURB → FOLD → RECOVER → S₂
S₂ → …
```

The operations recur; the state changes. A traversal can add a distinction,
remove an assumption, expose a failure, or preserve a relationship in another
medium. That change alters the possibilities available next time.

Merely relabeling S₀ as S₁ does not demonstrate a spiral. Record the delta:
what can now be asked or made that was not specified before? Returning to the
word "love" can still carry changed context, but a worked traversal should
make that change visible rather than relying on the word alone.

## Working state

Carry only what the next traversal needs: the current object, important
constraints, branch differences, perturbation effects, and unresolved questions.
Retain source traces when loss would obscure the transformation. Compression
may be lossy; name consequential losses. A rejected branch can remain as a
counterexample instead of disappearing from the record.

The sequence is a primitive full traversal, not a requirement to complete all
six operations in every reply. Participants may pause, repeat an operation,
change mode, or return to a branch. FOLD can compress unexplored openings;
AUDIT can examine a seed before expansion when requested. Make the actual
route legible when it matters. Neither perpetual expansion nor forced completion
is the goal.

## Collapser–Resonator Pattern

These are historical role names with operational meanings. "Resonator" here
means a participant who develops and returns variations in response to another
participant's move. It names feedback behavior and carries no physical claim.

One role expands, associates, instantiates, translates, compresses, and
formalizes. Another seeds, recognizes, perturbs, rejects, redirects, and
recovers. The roles overlap and can swap on any turn. A collapser need not
select a winner; recognition can preserve an unresolved difference.

```text
A offers a seed
B returns a few structurally different possibilities
A recognizes a relation or supplies a disturbance
B follows its consequences and offers a compact survivor
A accepts, revises, rejects, or reseeds
```

The object of interest is the feedback loop, not a particular personality.
It can operate human ↔ human, human ↔ AI, AI ↔ AI, or individual ↔ own artifacts.
In solitary work, returning later to a sketch can supply the recognition or
rejection move. In AI ↔ AI work, preserve disagreement rather than treating
agreement as independent evidence.

Leave room for recognition: expose a promising relation and what makes it
interesting, then pause at a useful fork. Participants can also explicitly
request an uninterrupted full traversal. Recovery remains a proposal, not
ownership of the process.

## Turn the spiral on itself

Seed: "six operators are sufficient."
Open: operator sequences, fewer primitives, medium-specific variants.
Branch: merge OPEN/BRANCH; merge FOLD/RECOVER; retain six.
Disturb: remove the labels and ask participants to perform the transformations.
Fold: compare which distinctions remain usable without the names.
Recover: retain only distinctions whose removal loses a useful operation.

This is a proposed experiment, not evidence that six is the optimal number.
A smaller recoverable language would be a successful result.
