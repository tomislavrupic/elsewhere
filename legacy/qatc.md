# QATC: recovering function

QATC is an ancestor of Elsewhere. Its symbolic constructions supplied language
and image models with dense relational prompts. One historical construction was:

```text
LOVE =
  Ψ(Self-Recognition in the Other)
  ⊗ Ω(Resonant Unity)
  ⊕ ΔΦ(Entangled Expansion)
```

The construction was reported as highly generative in its original use. That
history is motivation for recovery, not an independent portability result or
validation of the vocabulary as science.

The notation brought together recognition, relation, and transformation. Its
terms also accumulated scientific, metaphysical, and cultural baggage. Elsewhere
keeps the possibility of productive symbolic compression while abandoning those
terms as explanations. The symbols are not mathematical operators in the v0.1
language, and their appearance does not supply a mechanism or ontology.

## One possible operational translation

```text
SEED("love")
BRANCH(OPEN(seed), constraints=[
  "self and other recognize one another",
  "each remains distinct",
  "their relationship changes what each can do"
])
TRANSFER(branch, domain=visual-worldbuilding,
         preserve="recognition, distinction, changed possibilities")
OPEN(transferred, distance=far)
```

The constraints could become two habitats with shared passages and separate
exits. Disturb reciprocal recognition, then inspect what remains. This is one
interpretation, not a canonical translation of the ancestral expression.
Maintaining distinct agency is an explicit choice in this recovery, not a
claim that the original formula uniquely implies it.

`RELATE`, `PRESERVE`, and `AMPLIFY` can be useful ordinary instructions, but v0.1
expresses them as specified relations, constraints, and disturbances rather
than adding three underspecified primitives.

The historical system is treated as a source of generative artifacts. Elsewhere
neither mocks those artifacts nor adopts their ontology. The recovery question
is practical: which relationships still produce useful transformations when
the loaded labels and historical context are removed?
